Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

The short version: We do not collect, store, transmit, or have access to any of your personal data. Everything you enter into the app stays on your device. We never see it.

This Privacy Policy explains how Hoday ("the App," "we," "us") handles information. Hoday is developed by Paul-Justin Albeanu.

What the App does with your data

  • Storage: All data you create (activities, time entries, goals, preferences) is stored locally on your device only. It is never sent to us or to any server we operate, because we don't operate one.
  • No accounts: The App does not require or support creating an account. There is no sign-up, no login, and no user identity tied to your use of the App.
  • No analytics or tracking: We do not use analytics services, advertising identifiers, or any third-party tracking tools.
  • No third-party sharing: Because we don't collect any data, there is nothing for us to share, sell, or disclose to third parties.
  • Export and backup: The App lets you export your own data (as a JSON file) and import it back in. This is entirely under your control — we have no access to it at any point.

Notifications

If you enable notifications (for goals or timers), they are scheduled and delivered locally on your device. No notification content or scheduling data is sent to us.

Data collected by app store platforms

Apple's App Store and/or Google Play may independently collect limited technical data (such as crash reports or download statistics) as part of operating their platforms. This is governed by Apple's and Google's own privacy policies, not ours.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data from anyone at all. The App is not directed specifically at children.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change what data the App collects, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@hoday-timetracker.com

Developer note: if you later add ANY service that touches user data (crash reporting, analytics, cloud sync, etc.), this policy must be updated before that feature ships.
