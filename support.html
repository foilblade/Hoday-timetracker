Common questions

Is my data backed up anywhere?

No — Hoday stores everything locally on your device only. Use the Export feature in Settings to save a backup file, and Restore to bring it back in on a new device.

Why can't I delete a default activity?

Activities from Hoday's built-in catalog (like Sleep, Work, or Gym) can be archived — hiding them from your list — but not permanently deleted, so you can always bring them back. Anything you create yourself can be fully deleted.

I found a bug — what should I include when I email you?

A quick description of what happened, what you expected instead, and your device type (e.g. iPhone 15, Pixel 8) all help us fix it faster.
